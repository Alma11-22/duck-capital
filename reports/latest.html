How to read the score (0–100)

It starts from history: how often past selloffs of the same kind recovered. Then it adjusts. Insiders buying, analysts upgrading and healthy numbers push it up. Heavy debt, SEC red flags and missed earnings pull it down.

70+ Buy  The paper portfolio buys in three steps (now, −10%, −20%).

60–69 Watch  Interesting, but not yet.

Under 60  Not shown.

Psychology meter: is the drop about mood or numbers? High means the business still looks fine and the price fell anyway.

Sector selloff: the stock fell together with its whole sector, with no bad news of its own. A sector panic (numbers still healthy) has recovered about 70% of the time; a sector downturn (numbers weakening) about 35%; crypto about 40%. Small samples are weighted toward the 40% average.

The desk: six Claude analysts (news, fundamentals, bear, historian, event, risk) review the top names, then a portfolio-manager agent makes the call and can move the score by at most 15 points. The track record keeps "rules only" and "rules + desk" apart so you can see whether the desk adds value.
